FAQ

Straight answers

Is the audit really free?
Yes. It’s a one-page check from public Google information. There’s no obligation to buy anything after it.
Do you promise more jobs, better rankings or more reviews?
No. I don’t invent results, and I don’t promise a number of jobs, rankings or reviews. Google decides rankings and what shoppers see. Review requests go to every customer the same way, with no incentives and no filtering.
Is there a contract?
No. The monthly plan can be canceled anytime. The Profile Fix and the past-customer emails are one-time payments. If you cancel the monthly plan, service runs through the end of the period you’ve paid for; there are no partial refunds.
What is the founding rate?
The first 5 clients can subscribe at $197/month, and that rate stays in place while they remain subscribed. That plan includes a 30-day money-back guarantee on the first month, starting the day texts go live. The regular $297/month plan does not include that guarantee.
Will you text my past customers?
No. The $497 reactivation is email only. Marketing texts in the U.S. generally need prior written consent, and I don’t take that risk with a past-customer list.
Who are the emails from, and can people unsubscribe?
Reactivation emails go out from your email or email tool, with an unsubscribe link and your business address on every message, as U.S. commercial email rules require. Replies come to your inbox.
Does text-back replace answering the phone or handling emergencies?
No. Texts help your team follow up. They don’t replace picking up, dispatch, or telling someone to call 911 in an emergency.
How do I pay?
Checkout is on Whop, through the buttons on this page. Tax may apply. For the free audit, just email me — there’s nothing to pay.
